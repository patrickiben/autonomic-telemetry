01

The experience

The signal takes the screen. The controls wait nearby.

Work opens first, with cool blue, cyan, lavender, and pink light. Rest changes to jade, aqua, lime, and gold over a deep green field. The palette changes the atmosphere, not the interpretation of your body.

The initial source is Ambient: decorative movement with no device connected. Choose Try a demo to see explicitly synthetic variation. Choose Connect for real wearable input. No timer starts automatically.

Your personal estimate

The experimental inferred-arousal estimate compares Hume's reported heart rate and eligible Muse pulse-interval variability with an initial personal reference. Sit comfortably while the two-minute reference forms. On the 0–100 scale, 50 represents that reference: higher values reflect higher HR and/or lower pulse variability; lower values reflect the opposite pattern.

An explicitly labeled HR-only estimate uses its own reference when pulse variability is unavailable. Feature reasons and reference progress matter: raw packets can arrive before an estimate is ready. EEG is separate context with no estimate weight. The weights, stability floors, and display thresholds are heuristics, not validated physiological limits.

Open Reference & signals to inspect the reference, components, source, contribution, and recent history, or reset the reference. An unavailable estimate shows a dash, not zero. The estimate shapes the light's expansion, pace, and complexity alongside raw signal variation.

Work

Continuous observation, with an optional cue for a sustained elevation in your own estimate.

Rest

Paced breathing and a personal settling trend using the same estimate and reference.

This is an inference from a cardiac pattern, not direct sympathetic activity, a mental-state diagnosis, or a validated stress, attention, meditation, or health score. A change does not identify its cause. Formula, reference, and uncertainty

02

Muse and Hume

Muse raw EEG changes texture, optical PPG changes expansion, and motion changes distortion. Hume's reported heart rate changes the pace. These artistic mappings are separate from the gated feature extraction and personal estimate; the light's shape is not an extra score.

The bridge can extract eligible pulse-interval RMSSD from Muse optical data. This is PRV—pulse rate variability—not ECG-derived HRV. Motion, contact, breathing, and peak detection can affect it. Hume's reported rate does not supply individual pulse intervals or RMSSD.

When Hume is absent, accepted Muse pulse intervals can provide rate after two optical channels agree. Rate and PRV then share one sensor; they are not independent confirmations. A changed rate source rebuilds the personal reference.

Open Connect to choose a device. A nearby-device list appears when scanning through the Mac bridge. Select the actual wearable; the sheet shows connection status and a small recent-signal preview.

The Mac bridge detects Classic or newer Athena Muse transports. Direct Web Bluetooth on supported desktop Chromium or Chrome on Android is Classic-only; use the Mac for an updated Athena Muse S and for iPhone. Bounded physical tests passed on one Athena headband and one Hume band: 40 seconds of Muse raw streams and 90 seconds of Hume replies after warm-up, with clean disconnects. This does not establish every firmware's compatibility, indefinite operation, or physiological accuracy.

Apple Watch: separate Health context

You can still review or archive Watch data in Apple's Health app. A web page does not have native HealthKit access or a direct Watch Bluetooth feed here; a live route would need a native companion. HealthKit HRV is SDNN, distinct from RMSSD.

Apple's Health export guide explains Export All Health Data. The canvas does not import that XML; the separate Signal Lab can load it as retrospective context, never a live Watch feed or reference seed. See Watch notes.

03

On your iPhone

  1. On the Mac, open the repository's Start-iPhone-Bridge.command in Terminal. The first run installs the bridge dependencies.
  2. Keep the Mac awake, with both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi.
  3. In Connect on the Mac, scan the phone QR code with your iPhone camera and open the link in Safari. The printed phone address is a fallback.
  4. Use Connect to scan and select your actual Muse or Hume. If a vendor app holds a wearable, disconnect it there when you are ready to switch.

The Mac does the Bluetooth work. The phone receives signals from the local bridge, rather than from GitHub Pages. Your Mac and phone use the same experience, with integrated connection controls.

The local bridge uses unencrypted HTTP. Use trusted private Wi-Fi, keep the private link and QR code private, and do not expose its port to the internet.

Keep the source in view

BadgeMeaning

Live · Muse / HumeAccepted recent packets from the named device.
DemoExplicitly generated input, including bridge simulation.
Ambient · waiting for deviceConnecting or waiting for the first usable data.

When a stream stops, its influence fades toward ambient. The continuing light is not a continuing measurement. Fresh data from another connected device can still shape the canvas under its own source label.

Raw-signal variation is not proof of contact quality or a usable derived feature. Reported heart rate is not a series of measured heartbeat times. A Live badge can coexist with feature warm-up or reference collection. An HR-only estimate is explicitly distinct from the two-feature track.

05

Make it yours

Space offers less on-screen copy, gentler movement, a still image, richer or softer color, and full screen where supported. The system's reduced-motion preference starts the space with gentler movement.

Rest adds an optional four-second inhale and six-second exhale cue. It guides timing; it does not measure breathing or promise a response. A lower personal estimate is an observed trend, not proof of recovery. Breathing itself can alter pulse variability.

Work's Elevation cue starts off. If enabled, a gentle message appears after the estimate remains at least 67 for 60 seconds of valid data. Rest shows change since entering that mode and a 60-second trend; interrupted data or a changed source, track, or reference resets the comparison. The same inference drives both modes.

A timer, only if you want one

Timer offers 10, 25, or 50 minutes, or a custom duration from 1 to 360 minutes. Pause, resume, or end it whenever you choose. It follows real elapsed time independently of animation speed. When time is up, the space stays open.

06

Advanced Signal Lab

The separate Signal Lab keeps the detailed recording explorer: a fixed reference, transparent experimental pattern-shift index, novelty events, quality gates, descriptive diagnostics, markers, and import/export. Apple Health and dated Hume entries remain retrospective context.

It analyzes a labeled synthetic or user-supplied recording. Its one-sided pattern-shift index differs from the live canvas's 50-centered inferred-arousal estimate. Raw browser diagnostics, live derived features, and references do not silently cross between the two pages. Neither page grades a mental or health state.

Lab formulas, input contract, and limits

The Lab's Original research lenses have separate research engine definitions for prediction error, multivariate distance, covariance/PCA, entropy, cross-lags, and metrics requiring independently supplied RR intervals.

07

Data and methods

Raw packets, derived features, personal references, and visual settings stay in memory. There is no account, recording database, automatic browser saving, or cloud upload of readings. The immersive page has no recording import or session export; those remain explicit actions in the Signal Lab.

Software tests and simulation check code behavior. Bounded physical tests establish transport on the tested devices; they do not validate feature extraction or the inferred-arousal model. The estimate is calculated from eligible cardiac features, not from the canvas's colors.
