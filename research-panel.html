Research lenses · original mathematical tools

AR prediction errors, covariance distance, eigenvalue coupling, sample entropy, signed cross-lags, and a PCA trajectory. These experimental descriptors inspect the recording; they do not change the main index or the immersive visual.

Definitions and corrections ↗

Analysis runs when you choose it. No personal reference is saved automatically. 


  

    
Explicit RR intervals

    

      Open a JSON array, or an object with rr_intervals_ms,
      containing a consecutive sequence of independently supplied RR intervals
      in milliseconds. An artifact-corrected sequence is required for
      interpretation. The app does not create beat intervals from Hume heart
      rate or raw Muse PPG.
    

    
    

  

  

    
Recording adapters

    

      Mind Monitor bands require their declared power scale and timezone. Health
      Auto Export JSON remains dated context. Neither adapter fills missing HR,
      breathing, or variability channels.
