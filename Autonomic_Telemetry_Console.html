Waiting for usable signals

Keep your wearable on while a fixed two-minute reference is gathered. It stays fixed until you reset it.

The original multivariate architecture combines cardiac, respiratory, EEG, and covariance changes, with temporal-pattern modifiers. Zero is the reference origin; higher values reflect a stronger response under those terms. Covariance distance can respond to changes in either direction. Room to just be.

Keep this space beside your work, or let it fill the screen while you rest. You don’t have to do anything to use it.

  • Work watches your shift from your first 2 minutes continuously, with an optional sustained-elevation cue. Rest adds guided breathing and change since you entered. Both use the same estimate.
  • With wearables, eligible cardiac and EEG features feed a fixed-reference multivariate model. Covariance, temporal patterns, and causal surprise are calculated alongside the estimate. Respiratory input contributes when supplied. The estimate does not directly measure thoughts; the light does not show individual heartbeats.
  • Without a wearable, the light is ambient. Demo mode uses synthetic signals and is always labeled.
  • Your space stays local. This page sends no readings to a cloud service. A connected Mac relays nearby device data over your local network.
