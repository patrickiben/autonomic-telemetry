Your reference, your rhythm.

An experimental estimate of physiological activation relative to your own reference.

Waiting for usable signals

Keep your wearable on while a fixed two-minute reference is gathered. It stays fixed until you reset it.

50 marks your reference. Higher values reflect a higher heart rate and/or lower pulse variability relative to it; lower values reflect the opposite. This is not a direct measure of thoughts or a clinical score.

The light expands and becomes more active as the estimate rises. In Rest, the optional breathing guide adds a separate 4-second inhale / 6-second exhale rhythm.

Room to just be.

Keep this space beside your work, or let it fill the screen while you rest. You don’t have to do anything to use it.

  • Work monitors your personal arousal continuously, with an optional sustained-elevation cue. Rest adds guided breathing and change since you entered. Both use the same estimate.
  • With a wearable, usable heart rate and pulse variability build an experimental estimate against your fixed personal reference. Raw signal variation also adds texture. The estimate does not directly measure thoughts; the light does not show individual heartbeats.
  • Without a wearable, the light is ambient. Demo mode uses synthetic signals and is always labeled.
  • Your space stays local. This page sends no readings to a cloud service. A connected Mac relays nearby device data over your local network.
